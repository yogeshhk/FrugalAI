# Kitchen

## Dal tadka
Boil toor dal with turmeric until soft. For the tadka, heat ghee and add cumin seeds, garlic,
dried red chilli and curry leaves. Pour over the dal and add salt.

## Idli batter
Soak rice and urad dal separately for 6 hours, grind, mix, and let the batter ferment overnight.
Ferment for longer in winter.

# Frugal AI series: Medium stories and LinkedIn posts

Published: the Medium stories (Desi Stack publication) are out and the LinkedIn posts are scheduled (Oct 2026). This folder is the public record;
nothing here is private.

Files are paired by topic: `NN_topic_medium.md` (the story), `NN_topic_linkedin.md` (the post, banner prompt, alt text), `NN_topic_diagram.png`
(drawn by `_scripts/make_campaign_diagrams.py`). Topics: 01 umbrella, 02 quantum-wiki, 03 workflow-agent, 04 finetune-slm, 05 wiki-rag,
06 voice-form, 07 internships (no story; links all six).

## Rules the posts follow
- Plain, human register: short paragraphs, first person, contractions, no em dashes, no hype words.
- Story first; posts keep numbers out, stories carry a few with the device named, full tables stay in the project READMEs. A claim must be backed by the
  project README; a mixed result is reported as mixed. Code snippets are real excerpts; a shortened one says so.
- Never name a person other than the author, and never include personal contact details.

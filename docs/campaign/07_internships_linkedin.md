Facts behind this post: the project READMEs and `docs/internship-ideas.md`.

## Post

Over the last few weeks I shared the Frugal AI projects: a syllabus assistant, a workflow agent, fine-tuning on a laptop, a notes assistant, and a voice-filled form in Marathi and Hindi.

Every one of them is a working starting point, and none of them is finished. That's on purpose.

If you're a student, pick one. Each project folder has the code, the tests, the honest results and a list of what's still open. The repo also lists eleven more ideas, from offline speech recognition to plant-leaf disease checks to reading Modi script.

What I'd hope to see come out of it:
- internships and final-year projects that measure something real on ordinary hardware,
- research papers on small models in Indian languages, where there's a lot left to measure,
- apps that work in a village with no signal,
- and maybe a few startups.

The ground rules are simple. Run offline. Measure on a named machine. Report what didn't work as clearly as what did.

If your college or lab wants to run this as an internship batch, message me here.

# Post 1: What Frugal AI is (DRAFT)

## Post

Most AI demos I see assume three things: a fast internet connection, a cloud account, and a bill that grows with every question.

Many of the places I care about have none of the three. A school in a small town. A health worker's phone in a village. A college lab with laptops that are five years old.

So I started a small open-source repo called Frugal AI. The idea is simple: get useful AI out of the hardware people already have.

Small models that run on a plain laptop, with no GPU. No internet at run time, so data stays on the machine. No per-question token cost. And Indian languages, Marathi and Hindi first, because that's where the people are.

Every project in the repo does one real job, and every result in it was measured on a named machine. Where something didn't work, the README says so. Over the next few weeks I'll share them one at a time.

If you're a student looking for an internship or a thesis topic, a researcher who wants a paper out of this, or a startup that sees a product, each project is a starting point you can pick up.
